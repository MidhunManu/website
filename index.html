About Us

FlameStack is a technology and digital solutions company. We help businesses plan, build and run the systems they rely on every day.

  • We understand your business first, then choose the technology.
  • We build systems that are easy to maintain and extend.
  • We stay with you after launch for hosting, support and updates.

Most businesses work with separate vendors for their brand, website, software, hosting and marketing. At FlameStack, one team covers all of it, so each part works with the rest. You can hire us for one service, or for a complete solution from idea to launch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with us

What kind of businesses do you work with?

We work with businesses of different sizes, from companies building their first website or app to established teams upgrading their systems.

How does a project start?

We start with a conversation about your goals. After that, we send a proposal with the scope, timeline and cost before any work begins.

Can you work with our existing software?

Yes. We can extend your current systems, connect them together, or replace them step by step.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, hosting, monitoring and updates based on what each project needs.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on the scope, complexity and timeline of the project. You get a clear quote before work starts.
